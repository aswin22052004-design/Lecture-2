Our Story

Built by Musicians,
for Musicians

We got tired of sending files over email, losing track of which version was final, and arguing over royalty splits. So we built the platform we wished existed.

Mission

Democratize Music Creation

Our mission is to give every musician — from bedroom producers to major label artists — the professional collaboration tools they deserve, without the enterprise price tag.

We believe that the best music comes from genuine collaboration, and that collaboration should be frictionless, transparent, and fair.
