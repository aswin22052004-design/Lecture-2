Our Story

Built by Musicians,
for Musicians

Music Collaboration Studio was born from the frustration of sending files over email, losing track of versions, and arguing over royalties. We built the platform we wished existed.

Today, thousands of artists, producers, and engineers use MCS to turn ideas into finished tracks — with full transparency, clear agreements, and zero friction.

  • Professional-grade tools at every level
  • Transparent royalty tracking from day one
  • Legally-sound digital agreements
  • Your data is yours — always encrypted
  • Built with musicians on every design decision
